Category E: Procedural Vulnerability & AccessibilityThese tags ensure the system adapts to the user's constraints without deprioritizing their case.   tag_name: Priority_Disability -> Flags the applicant for priority legal aid access as mandated by the Rights and Protection of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2013.   tag_name: PWD_Visual -> Forces the system to utilize voice-only or screen-reader accessible routes.   tag_name: Restricted_Contact -> Engages a Zero-Outbound SMS lock and records the specific safe window for a discreet human callback.   tag_name: Proxy_Applicant -> Indicates the intake is second-hand information, requiring subsequent direct verification.   tag_name: Incomplete_NID -> Defers strict identity requirements for vulnerable citizens whose documents are inaccessible.   Category F: System Integrity & Routing (The "Meta-Tags")To make these laws function seamlessly within the DLAO operational database, every extracted tag must be accompanied by the following three database schema integration tags.   COMPOUNDABLE_STATUS: Maps the case as either [Mediation Allowed] under Section 345 of the CrPC and Section 21B of the Legal Aid Services Act, or [Court Filing Required] if it is non-compoundable.   JURISDICTION_LEVEL: Automatically routes the case queue to the [Family Court], [Civil Court], [Magistrate Court], or [Labour Tribunal] based on the laws engaged.   AUTO_TEMPLATE: Directly connects the applied legal act to pre-filled system documents, such as a Notice of Hearing, Summons, or a Solenama (Settlement Deed).
